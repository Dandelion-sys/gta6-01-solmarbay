SOLMAR BAY

An open-world crime story on the sunny coast

You are Ian, fresh out of the harbour district and broke. Fixer Marisol has work for you — if you can handle the heat. Your partner Ryan is out there too — the two of you are a couple, taking Solmar Bay back together, one job at a time. Press C to switch between them. Steal cars, outrun the Solmar Bay Police, and climb from street hustler to king of the bay.
